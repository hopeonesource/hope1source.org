# Corporate sponsorship and volunteer outreach sources

Reviewed October 15, 2026 for T145. The user requested corporate volunteer outreach for community connection and team building, a linked background page, and examples including Adobe and Cisco. The new page is `/corporate-sponsors` in v4 only; publication remains pending PR review.

## Company examples

- **Adobe:** Identified as a partner by the user in this request. A bounded public search and review of existing H1S articles did not locate an independent dated Adobe/H1S source. The page includes Adobe in the historical partner/supporter list based on the user's confirmation. It does not assign an activity, date, donation, outcome, or current sponsorship to Adobe. Adobe's general volunteering webpage is not cited as evidence of an H1S relationship.
- **Cisco Systems and Lockr Web Encryption:** H1S's September 13, 2018 Hurricane Florence release names both as supporters of the volunteer-led effort. The new page links to the existing full internal article, `/press/hurricane-florence-alerts`. Original retained source: https://web.archive.org/web/20190819210945/https://www.hopeonesource.org/news/hopeonesource-hurricane-florence-press-release . The January 19, 2017 release also names Tropo (a Cisco company) and Lockr Encryption; internal article: `/press/derrick-woodfork-resources`.
- **Blackstone Federal:** Blackstone Technology Group's June 13, 2017 release announces a strategic partnership with Hope With Love. It describes employee participation in humanitarian code sprints and ongoing volunteer contributions to H1S. Source: https://www.prweb.com/releases/blackstone_federal_joins_the_fight_to_prevent_and_end_homelessness_in_the_nation_s_capital/prweb14414915.htm . The new page summarizes this account and links directly to it.
- **Community outreach:** Blackstone Technology Group's October 19, 2017 release announces joint Veterans Day outreach with H1S and the DC Mayor's Office of Veterans Affairs to register people for service information and emergency alerts. Source: https://www.prweb.com/releases/saving_lives_in_the_nation_s_capital_one_text_at_a_time/prweb14819980.htm . The new page uses “announced,” not a claim that the planned event was completed or reached a particular number of people.

## Wording and scope

- Community connection and team building are presented as opportunities and purposes for volunteering, not measured outcomes or guaranteed benefits.
- Prospective activities are invitations to explore a partnership. No fixed event package, current corporate endorsement, company-specific volunteering history for Adobe/Cisco/Lockr, or employee headcount is invented.
- IEEE and AmeriCorps are not listed as companies. No company logos or additional images are added.
- About retains its other four audience bullets and approved copy. The new page keeps the requested `https://hopeonesource.me` destination available. Get Involved gains one corporate-sponsor card; existing card wording is unchanged. The legacy `/our-sponsors` alias now opens this relevant new page.
